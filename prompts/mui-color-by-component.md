# Fold the MUI rulings into your mapping

Read `docs/color-by-component-mui.md` and fold its rows into the mapping you are building
from the set onto MUI: a row marked ruled wins; a row marked derived you may replace, and
you list the row, your answer and your reason for the operator. Before relying on a row,
check that its variable exists in `dist/brands/<brand>/web/variables.css` here, and list
any that does not with the nearest existing name. Do not resolve a disagreement or a
missing name yourself: list them, print the list, and stop. Change nothing in this
repository.
