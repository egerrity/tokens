# Merge the color engine into the generator

Read `docs/color-engine-merge.md` and work by it, one stage per request: the operator
starts you with "Follow this file, stage 1 only" and names each later stage. A stage
ends when its "working looks like" holds; where it cannot, stop and say why, and patch
nothing around it. The copy comes from the throwaway repository the operator names, at the
tag its `VERSION` file states; take nothing from anywhere else. Do not resolve an open
ruling yourself: list what it blocks and stop. No commits without the operator's word, no
pushes, and the Figma stage runs on a copy of a file, never the library.
